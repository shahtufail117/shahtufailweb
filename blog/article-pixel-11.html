Pixel 11 specs and price leak with no surprises

👤 Terrence O'Brien 📅 Aug 1, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read Source: The Verge

Android Headlines claims to have the specs and price for the entire Pixel 11 lineup. What the site shared basically lines up with everything else that we've heard in the lead-up to the August 12th event.

The Pixel 11 is expected to get a $100 price hike, starting at $899, but will come with 256GB of storage instead of 128GB in the base model. While the entire lineup will sport the new Tensor G6 and Titan M3 chips, the base model Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL are both getting that rumored RAM cut to just 12GB, along with price increases to $1,099 and $1,299, respectively.

More confirmation that price hikes and RAM cuts are coming our way.

The Pixel 11 will supposedly ditch the telephoto lens and settle for a dual rear camera setup instead. The pro models appear like they'll keep the same camera setup from the Pixel 10 lineup. The displays also seem to be largely unchanged from the previous generation.

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold wasn't immune to RAMageddon, either, and will also start with 12GB of RAM and a new price of $1,899.

  • Pixel 11: $899, 256GB storage, dual rear camera
  • Pixel 11 Pro: $1,099, 12GB RAM
  • Pixel 11 Pro XL: $1,299, 12GB RAM
  • Pixel 11 Pro Fold: $1,899, 12GB RAM

The price increases across the lineup are being driven by the ongoing memory shortage, which has sent RAM and storage costs soaring across the industry.

This article is based on reporting by Terrence O'Brien, published on The Verge on Aug 1, 2026.
