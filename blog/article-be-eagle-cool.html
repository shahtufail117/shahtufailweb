Eagle.cool - The Design Asset Management Tool Every Designer Needs

👤 Tufail Shah 📅 Aug 25, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read Source:

As a graphic designer managing hundreds of visual assets daily, I needed a better way to organize and find files. After testing multiple solutions, I found Eagle.cool - an asset management app that completely transformed my day-to-day workflow.

Standard file explorers, especially on Windows, make it tedious to browse through media assets. Formats like Adobe Illustrator (.EPS), Photoshop (.PSD), and vector PDFs rarely generate thumbnails by default, and searching through generic icon folders slows down your momentum when you need to find an asset quickly.

Workarounds I Tested

Before finding Eagle, I tested several popular file and image management tools. SageThumbs automatically generates thumbnail previews in Windows Explorer for various design formats, but the software is old and no longer actively maintained. Newer file formats often fail to load, and you must manually enable "Save Thumbnail/Preview" inside Illustrator or Photoshop for it to work.

XnView is a fast, free alternative to Adobe Lightroom that supports library browsing, search, batch conversions, size reduction, and color adjustments. While it is an incredible free option, it lacks modern workflow integrations tailored specifically for creative production. I also tested Files for Windows, XYplorer, and ACDSee Photo Manager, but none nailed the exact balance of speed and design-focused features I needed.

Why Eagle Won Me Over

Eagle topped every other software I tested because of a few standout features. Virtual Files and Folders let you drag and drop files into Eagle, where it organizes them in an isolated, dedicated environment. You can choose to link to original files or let Eagle manage separate copies inside its library for seamless organization.

Extensive Format and Preview Support means Eagle native-previews almost everything: images, videos, 3D models, PDFs, and vector files. No more guessing which file is the real one when you have five versions named Final_Final_v2.psd.

Built-in Plugins and Quick Actions eliminate the need to boot up Photoshop for basic edits. Eagle features built-in tools for background removal, AI-powered visual and reverse-image search, image cropping and compression, and screen color picking.

Community Content and Offline Assets let you download community-contributed icon packs, illustrations, concept art, fonts, and mockups directly into the app for offline access. You can also export your whole library as a .eagle file to move your workspace across machines.

Eagle is a powerhouse for designers looking to cut visual clutter and find assets fast.

Smart Folders

Set rules based on tags, file types, dimensions, or colors, and Smart Folders automatically filter and organize your assets in real time as you add them. This single feature alone saves hours of manual sorting every week.

Final Verdict

Eagle is a powerhouse for designers looking to cut visual clutter and find assets fast. Pricing is about $35 USD (one-time payment) as of writing, and the license covers up to 2 PCs or Macs, with easy device management built directly into the app.
